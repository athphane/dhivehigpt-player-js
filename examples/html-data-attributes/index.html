Responsive layout

The bar measures its own container width (CSS container queries, not the viewport). Below 430px it switches to two rows aligned to the play button; below 260px the credit text and volume slider drop to icon-only.

Sticky-to-bottom while playing

Press play, then scroll — the bar slides up and sticks to the bottom of the screen, and stays there through pause. Use the stop button to dismiss it.
